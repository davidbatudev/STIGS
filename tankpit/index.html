How to play — Left-click the field to drive there (or WASD). Aim with the mouse, fire with Space / right-click. M lay mine · R radar · T teleport (overhead map) · Tab scoreboard. Fuel is your health: moving, shooting, mines, radar, teleporting and hits all burn it. Grab hidden fuel canisters and equipment (cannon, shield, long radar). Ride ferries across water, push crates. Rank from Recruit up to General.
